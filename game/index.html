EMBER DUEL

Cards of the Four Winds

A duel of embers and tides. Outplay Rook, the Stone Warden —
reduce his life to 0 before he reduces yours.

🔥 Flame — strikes the foe
💧 Tide — heals your wounds
🍃 Leaf — raises a shield
🪨 Stone — heavy, crushing blows

Each turn you gain mana and draw a card. Play cards, then press End Turn.
First to 0 life loses. Good luck, Ember Caller.

Mouse + keyboard · 1–7 play a card · Enter ends the turn · R restarts
🏆

VICTORY!

Rook crumbles to embers. The Four Winds sing your name,
Ember Caller — the table is yours.

💀

DEFEAT

Rook stands unmoved as your last ember fades.
The winds fall silent — but a Caller never stays down.

How to play

  • Goal: reduce Rook's life from 30 to 0 before he does the same to you.
  • Mana: each turn you gain mana crystals (turn 1 = 1, up to 10). Cards cost mana.
  • Cards: 🔥 Flame deals damage · 💧 Tide heals you · 🍃 Leaf gives you shield (absorbs damage) · 🪨 Stone deals heavy damage.
  • Shield absorbs damage first, then breaks. Healing cannot exceed 30 life.
  • Ember Surge adds +5 to your next attack this turn. Phoenix Feather and Deep Current draw an extra card.
  • End Turn lets Rook play. He draws, gains mana and plays his best cards — watch his shield.
  • Keys: 1–7 play a card, Enter ends the turn, R restarts, M toggles sound, H shows this help.
